CivicNeuro resident app — screen flow map
Every real screen from the mockup, arranged by cluster with arrows showing what each button, tap, or nav item leads to. Scroll to explore; lines are color-coded by flow type. Updated: dangling links fixed (forgot password, edit profile, other polls, alert deep-links, trending drill-in); password reset uses an emailed link; signup now requires email confirmation before Home; guests can choose SMS or email updates and can re-enter tracking from Welcome; report creation adds duplicate-detection, location permission + pin-drop, media capture, and offline queueing; report closure adds a satisfaction check and reopen/escalate flow (escalation triggers on SLA breach or manual request); session expiry, sign-out confirmation, notification granularity, and a POPIA data-export option before deletion are now shown; empty/error states are shown as a representative pattern to be applied to every screen; Beacon's "Add to your circle" now searches CivicNeuro residents by name/number instead of syncing phone contacts, dropping the native contacts-permission step entirely. v4: the Menu button now sits top-left of every signed-in screen (the old bottom-nav Menu slot is gone); that slot is now Lifeline — four preset cards (Crime, Fire, Sickness, GBV) plus cards residents create themselves, each one a group of saved phone numbers that all ring when the card is tapped (3-second cancel window, live-location text sent alongside). Unlike Beacon, Lifeline does ask for contacts permission, because it is built on the resident's saved numbers. v5: Snap & Say is now the hero on Home — the resident speaks or types, snaps a photo, and an AI agent turns it into a report (category, keywords like water/fire/theft, description, location, evidence) that they confirm before it is routed to the right organisation; the manual form is still one tap away. Emergency wording interrupts with a Lifeline call, and unclear reports get one follow-up question. v6: Home's first row is now two cards — Snap & Say and Report an issue — above the Your area card; the Safety and Help tiles are removed from Home because both already live in the Menu. v7: Lifeline and Beacon now share ONE contacts permission — whichever feature needs contacts first shows a single explainer + the OS prompt; the other feature then skips straight to its contacts screen. If the resident declines, both keep working (type a number / search by name or number). Permissions are still asked in context, not during sign-up.

Privacy policy
Last updated 12 August 2026
Data we collect
Your name, contact details, and the reports, messages and evidence you submit, plus location data when you choose to share it while reporting an issue.
How we use it
To route your reports to the right organisation, keep you updated on their progress, and — only in aggregate, never identifying you — to show community-wide trends like Community Pulse.
Who can see it
Only the organisation you report to can see your name and contact details for that report. Platform administrators can access data to keep the service running safely.
Your rights
You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or ask us to delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account.
Contact us
Our designated Information Officer handles all requests about your personal information under POPIA. Email privacy@civicneuro.org or write to the Information Officer, CivicNeuro, for access, correction, or deletion requests.

Terms & conditions
Last updated 12 August 2026
Using CivicNeuro
CivicNeuro lets you report civic issues to the organisation responsible for your area and track their response. You must provide accurate information when submitting a report.
Acceptable use
Reports must be genuine. Submitting false, malicious, or duplicate reports may result in your account being suspended.
Organisation responses
CivicNeuro connects you with organisations but isn't responsible for how quickly they act, or for the outcome of any report.
Your account
You're responsible for keeping your login details secure. You can delete your account at any time from Account.
Changes to these terms
We'll notify you in-app if these terms change in a way that affects how your information is used.
Governing law
These terms are governed by South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
